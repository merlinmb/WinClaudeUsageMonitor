# Design, Theme, Colours and UX Language for App

## Design

The design of this app follows a modern, minimalist aesthetic with clean lines and plenty of white space to create a visually appealing and uncluttered experience. The app is designed to be intuitive and easy to use, with clear labels and navigation options.

## Theme

The theme of the app is minimalistic and focused on functionality. The color palette is limited to neutral tones such as black, white, gray, and navy blue, which creates a professional and trustworthy appearance. The font used throughout the app is sans-serif, which provides easy readability and a clean, modern look.

## Colours

- Black: #000000
- White: #FFFFFF
- Gray: #808080
- Navy Blue: #001F3F

## UX Language

The UX language used in the app is straightforward and concise. All labels, buttons, and navigation options are clearly labeled to provide a seamless and intuitive user experience. The language used throughout the app is also consistent, which helps users quickly learn and understand how to use the app.
